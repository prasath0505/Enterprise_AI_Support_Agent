Title: Company Password Management and Self-Service Reset Policy
Classification: Internal Corporate Use Only
Category: Security
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1.0 OVERVIEW
This document defines the strict security standards and operational procedures governing corporate user accounts. Maintaining robust credential hygiene is critical to safeguarding company infrastructure, intellectual property, and client data against unauthorised access. 

2.0 SELF-SERVICE PORTAL ACCESS
Employees can reset their corporate password through the internal password portal. This self-service platform is accessible inside the corporate network or via a secure web browser at https://enterprise.corp. Employees must set up their security profile—including enrolling a mobile device for Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) and configuring three secret challenge questions—during their initial onboarding week.

3.0 PASSWORD COMPLEXITY CRITERIA
To protect against automated brute-force attacks, simple phrases or dictionary words are banned by the directory controller. Your new password must contain the following structural properties:
- A minimum length of fourteen (14) total characters.
- At least one uppercase alphabetic character (A-Z).
- At least one lowercase alphabetic character (a-z).
- At least one numerical digit (0-9).
- At least one special cryptographic character (e.g., !, @, #, $, %, ^, *, _, +).
- No historical overlap: You cannot reuse any of your last twelve (12) previous passwords.
- No personal identifiers: Passwords cannot contain your name, birthdate, or sequential patterns (like "12345" or "abcd").

4.0 PASSWORD EXPIRATION SCHEDULE
To limit exposure windows from potential credential harvesting, your password expires every ninety (90) calendar days. Automated system alerts will begin appearing on your workstation and via your corporate email starting fourteen (14) days prior to the enforcement date. If an employee fails to update their credentials before the countdown hits zero, the account status shifts to disabled, blocking email, file shares, and application access.

5.0 LOGISTICAL EXCEPTIONS & LOCKOUTS
If an employee cannot access the portal due to an expired credential, a lost MFA authentication device, or a localized network blackout, they cannot bypass system prompts independently. Entering an invalid credential five (5) consecutive times triggers a mandatory intruder-defense lockout status, rendering the account inactive for thirty (30) minutes before auto-unlocking.

6.0 ESCALATION PATHWAY
Contact the IT service desk immediately if your self-service profile fails, or if an urgent workflow requires an immediate manual override during an active account lock. Technicians will perform a mandatory voice-validation protocol to verify your employee identity before dispatching a temporary, single-use bypass token.
